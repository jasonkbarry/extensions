Nextiva Login Email Picker

On the Nextiva Contact Center sign-in screen, pick which login email to use with one click. It fills the Email field and moves you to the password. It starts empty, so you add your own emails.

Install or update with the installer (Windows)

  1. Option A: press Win+R, paste this line, and press Enter: 
    powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "$x='nextiva-email-picker-blank'; irm https://jasonkbarry.github.io/extensions/install.txt | iex"

    Option B: download Install-EmailPicker.zip, right-click it and choose Extract All, then double-click the .cmd file inside. If Windows warns you, click More info → Run anyway.
  2. First time: it installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Nextiva Extensions\nextiva-email-picker-blank, copies that path to your clipboard, and opens chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked. In the folder window, click the address bar, press Ctrl+V then Enter, and click Select Folder.
  3. Updates: run it again. It finds where Nextiva Login Email Picker is already installed in Chrome or Edge, even a folder you chose yourself, and copies the new version there with your settings kept. Then click the reload arrow on Nextiva Login Email Picker in chrome://extensions.

Install manually

  1. Download the zip, then right-click it and choose Extract All. Move the extracted nextiva-email-picker-blank folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents. Chrome keeps loading it from there.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the nextiva-email-picker-blank folder.
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Nextiva Login Email Picker so it's easy to reach.

Use

  • Open the Nextiva sign-in page. The picker opens by itself, or click the ✉ button in the Email field or the toolbar icon.
  • Click + Add email to add a login: partner, tenant name and email.
  • Click a name to fill the Email field. Delete email at the bottom removes one.

Your emails are saved in Chrome on your own computer and never leave it. Don't move or rename the extension folder after loading it, or Chrome treats it as a new extension and the list starts empty again.

Updating

Easiest: run the installer again (Win+R command or zip above), then click the reload arrow in chrome://extensions. Or by hand: Download the new zip, extract it, and copy its files over your existing folder. Then click the reload arrow on the extension in chrome://extensions. Your saved emails are kept.

Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
