Nextiva Login Email Picker

On the Nextiva Contact Center sign-in screen, pick which login email to use with one click. It fills the Email field and moves you to the password. It starts empty, so you add your own emails.

nextiva-email-picker-blank-1.3.1.zip · 17 KB
Download

Install

  1. Download the zip, then right-click it and choose Extract All. Move the extracted nextiva-email-picker-blank folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents. Chrome keeps loading it from there.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the nextiva-email-picker-blank folder.
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Nextiva Login Email Picker so it's easy to reach.

Use

  • Open the Nextiva sign-in page. The picker opens by itself, or click the ✉ button in the Email field or the toolbar icon.
  • Click + Add email to add a login: partner, tenant name and email.
  • Click a name to fill the Email field. Delete email at the bottom removes one.

Your emails are saved in Chrome on your own computer and never leave it. Don't move or rename the extension folder after loading it, or Chrome treats it as a new extension and the list starts empty again.

Updating

Download the new zip, extract it, and copy its files over your existing folder. Then click the reload arrow on the extension in chrome://extensions. Your saved emails are kept.

Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
