Contact Center Guard

Protects calls on the Nextiva Contact Center agent screen.

contact-center-guard-1.6.zip · 6 KB
Download

Install

  1. Download the zip, then right-click it and choose Extract All. Move the extracted contact-center-guard folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents. Chrome keeps loading it from there.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the contact-center-guard folder.
  4. Check that Contact Center Guard is turned on. It has no toolbar button; it works on its own on the agent screen.

What it does

  • Back: always blocked on the agent screen (toolbar Back, Alt+Left, mouse back button), with a popup explaining why.
  • Refresh: F5 or Ctrl+R shows a warning first. Press it again within a minute to refresh anyway.
  • Callbacks: on an Outbound Call / In review, choosing a disposition first shows a reminder to call the customer back. Try again to continue without calling.

Closing the tab, typing a new address and Chrome's toolbar Reload button are not blocked. If you also have Callback Disposition Guard installed, turn it off; this extension includes its callback reminder.

Updating

Download the new zip, extract it, and copy its files over your existing folder. Then click the reload arrow on the extension in chrome://extensions.

Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
