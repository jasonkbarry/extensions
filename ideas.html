How evaluation works

  • Every idea starts as New. The team adds evaluation notes as comments on the idea in GitHub, covering effort, impact, approach and questions. Those notes appear in its Evaluation section here.
  • A status label shows where it stands: New → Evaluating → Planned → Building → Done, or Not now.
  • Agree with an idea? Open it and add a 👍 reaction. "Most 👍" sorts by votes.
Ideas are stored as GitHub issues in jasonkbarry/extensions, so they're public. Don't include customer data, passwords or other confidential details.
