How evaluation works

  • Every idea starts as New. The team adds notes in its Evaluation, covering effort, impact, approach and questions, and moves the status along: New → Evaluating → Planned → Building → Done, or Not now.
  • Agree with an idea? Click 👍 on it. "Most 👍" sorts by votes.
Ideas are listed publicly on this page. Please don't include customer data, passwords or other confidential details. Ideas and screenshots are stored in the team's Google Sheet and Drive.
